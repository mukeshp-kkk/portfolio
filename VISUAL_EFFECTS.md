## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The CSS enhancements follow these principles:

1. **Purposeful Motion**
   - Every animation serves a purpose
   - Draws attention to important elements
   - Provides feedback for interactions
   - Enhances rather than distracts

2. **Performance First**
   - GPU-accelerated transforms
   - Efficient keyframes
   - Reduced motion support
   - Minimal repaints

3. **Black & White Excellence**
   - No colors added
   - Sophisticated through motion
   - Typography and spacing focused
   - Gradient effects in grayscale

4. **Accessibility**
   - Respects reduced motion preferences
   - Keyboard navigation enhanced
   - Focus indicators clear
   - Screen reader friendly
